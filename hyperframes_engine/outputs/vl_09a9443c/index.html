While many foods contain Vitamin C, we can broadly group them into categories. This helps us understand the different 'types' of sources more clearly.

Vitamin C: Fresh vs. Cooked

Fresh & Raw
Highest Vitamin C content
Nutrients are best preserved
Examples: Raw salads, fresh fruit juices
Minimize exposure to heat and air
Cooked & Processed
Vitamin C can decrease significantly
Sensitive to heat, light, and water
Examples: Boiled vegetables, overcooked foods
Steaming or quick stir-frying is better
An important fact about Vitamin C is its sensitivity. How we prepare our food can significantly impact how much of this vital nutrient we actually consume.
