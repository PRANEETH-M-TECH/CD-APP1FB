What is a Simple Electric Circuit?

The Path of Electricity!

Hello, young scientists! Today, we're going to explore something amazing that powers many things around us: a simple electric circuit!
Simple Electric Circuit
Battery (Power Source)
Connecting Wires
Bulb (Load)
Switch (Optional)
A simple circuit has a few important parts: a power source like a battery, wires to connect everything, and a device like a bulb that uses electricity.

How Electricity Flows in a Circuit

Step 1
1. Electricity leaves the Battery (+ terminal)
Step 2
2. Travels through the Connecting Wire
Step 3
3. Passes through the Bulb (making it glow)
Step 4
4. Returns to the Battery (- terminal)
Step 5
5. Completes the Closed Loop!
🔄 Cycle Flow
Electricity needs a complete path to flow. It starts from the battery, goes through the wire, lights up the bulb, and returns to the battery, forming a loop!

Open vs. Closed Circuits

Closed Circuit
Complete path for electricity.
Electricity flows.
Bulb glows (device works).
Switch is ON.
Open Circuit
Broken path for electricity.
Electricity cannot flow.
Bulb does not glow (device off).
Switch is OFF.
When the path is complete, it's a closed circuit and the bulb glows. If the path is broken, it's an open circuit, and the bulb stays off.

Circuit in Action: Before & After

Before: Open Circuit
Wires disconnected
No electricity flow
Bulb is OFF
➔
After: Closed Circuit
Wires connected
Electricity flows
Bulb is ON!
See how the bulb is off when the circuit is open? But when we connect everything properly, the circuit closes, and *poof*! The bulb lights up!
