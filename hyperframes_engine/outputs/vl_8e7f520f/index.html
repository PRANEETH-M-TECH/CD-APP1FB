The journey of wool involves several important steps. It's like a magical transformation! Let's look at the main stages of how wool goes from an animal to a comfy sweater.

Step 1: Shearing - A Gentle Haircut!

Before Shearing
Sheep with thick, heavy fleece.
Keeps the sheep warm in winter.
➔
After Shearing
Fleece carefully removed.
Ready for washing and processing.
The very first step is called shearing. Imagine giving the sheep a gentle haircut! It's done without hurting the sheep, usually in warm weather.

Steps 2 & 3: Cleaning and Sorting the Fleece

2. Scouring (Washing)
Washing the shorn fleece in large tanks.
Removes dirt, grease (lanolin), and sweat.
Makes the wool clean and bright.
3. Sorting (Grading)
Separating wool based on texture and quality.
Finer wool for clothes, coarser for carpets.
Removes 'burrs' (small knots) from the wool.
After shearing, the raw wool needs a good clean and then careful sorting. These steps make sure we get good quality wool ready for spinning.

Steps 4-6: From Fibre to Fabric

StepActionResult
4. DyeingCleaned wool fibres are dipped in coloured dyes.Vibrant, colourful wool fibres.
5. SpinningColoured fibres are straightened and twisted tightly together.Long, strong wool yarn (thread).
6. Weaving/KnittingYarn is woven on looms or knitted into fabric.Warm woollen cloth, sweaters, or blankets!
Once the wool is clean and sorted, it's time for some magic! It gets colored, spun into yarn, and then woven into the fabric we know.
