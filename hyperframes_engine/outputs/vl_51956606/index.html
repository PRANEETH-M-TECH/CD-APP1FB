Essential Nutrient

Vitamin C, also known as ascorbic acid, is an essential nutrient our bodies can't produce. It's a powerful antioxidant, crucial for protecting our cells and boosting our immune system. Think of it as your body's shield!

Main Sources of Vitamin C

Fruits
Often sweet or tart
Many can be eaten raw
Known for high water content
Examples: Citrus, Berries, Tropical Fruits
Vegetables
Diverse in taste and texture
Can be eaten raw or cooked
Often rich in fiber and other vitamins
Examples: Leafy Greens, Peppers, Cruciferous Veggies
So, where can we find this amazing nutrient? Generally, Vitamin C is abundant in two major food groups: fruits and vegetables. Let's look at some key characteristics of each.
