The Power of Proteins

Building Blocks for Your Body

Hello young scientists! Today, we're going to learn about a super important nutrient called proteins. They are like the building blocks of our body!
Proteins
Help in Growth
Repair Body Tissues

Proteins do many amazing things for us! They help us grow tall and strong, repair our body when we get hurt, and even fight off sickness.
