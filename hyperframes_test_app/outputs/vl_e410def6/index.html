Discovering Acids and Bases: The Test!

How to tell an Acid from a Base?

Hello young scientists! Have you ever wondered how we know if a substance is an acid or a base without tasting it? Today, we're going to explore exciting tests for this!

First, let's understand what acids and bases generally are. Acids often taste sour, like a lemon, but can be harmful. Bases often feel slippery, like soap, and taste bitter.
Indicators
Special Substances
Change Color
Since tasting is not safe for many substances, we use special 'detectives' called indicators! These amazing substances change color to tell us if something is an acid or a base.

If it's an ACID...
Blue litmus paper turns RED.
Red litmus paper stays RED.
Think: A for Acid, A for Alarm (Red)!
If it's a BASE...
Red litmus paper turns BLUE.
Blue litmus paper stays BLUE.
Think: B for Base, B for Blue!
One of the most popular indicators is litmus paper! It comes in two colors: blue and red. Let's see what happens when we dip them into acids and bases.
