Perimeter vs. Area: Square and Rectangle Fields

Which field has a larger area?

Welcome to our lesson on comparing geometric shapes! Today, we're presented with an interesting challenge: two fields, one square and one rectangular, both share the exact same perimeter. Our mission is to determine which of these fields offers a larger area. Let's dive in!

1. Perimeter of the Square Field

1
Side length (s): 60 m
2
Perimeter formula: P = 4s
3
Substitute value: P = 4 × 60 m
4
Calculate perimeter: P = 240 m
5
Square Perimeter: 240 m
Let's begin with the square field. We know its side length is 60 meters. To find its perimeter, we simply multiply the side length by four, since all four sides are equal. This calculation gives us the total length of the boundary around our square field.

2. Finding the Width of the Rectangular Field

1
Given Perimeter (P): 240 m
2
Given Length (l): 80 m
3
Perimeter formula: P = 2(l + w)
4
Substitute knowns: 240 = 2(80 + w)
5
Divide by 2: 120 = 80 + w
6
Solve for w: w = 120 - 80
7
Width (w): 40 m
8
Rectangle Width: 40 m
Now, for the rectangular field. We are given its length is 80 meters, and crucially, it has the same perimeter as the square: 240 meters. Using the perimeter formula for a rectangle, P equals two times (length plus width), we can substitute our known values and solve for the missing width.

3. Area of the Square Field

1
Side length (s): 60 m
2
Area formula: A = s × s
3
Substitute value: A = 60 m × 60 m
4
Calculate area: A = 3600 m²
5
Square Area: 3600 m²
With the perimeter handled, let's turn our attention to the area. For the square field, with a side length of 60 meters, the area is calculated by multiplying the side by itself. This gives us the total ground covered by the square field in square meters.

4. Area of the Rectangular Field

1
Length (l): 80 m
2
Width (w): 40 m
3
Area formula: A = l × w
4
Substitute values: A = 80 m × 40 m
5
Calculate area: A = 3200 m²
6
Rectangle Area: 3200 m²
Now, we find the area of the rectangular field. We know its length is 80 meters and we've just calculated its width to be 40 meters. The area of a rectangle is simply length multiplied by width. Let's perform this final calculation.

Comparison: Which field has a larger area?

Square Field
Perimeter: 240 m
Area: 3600 m²
The Square Field has a larger area (3600 m² > 3200 m²).
Rectangular Field
Perimeter: 240 m
Area: 3200 m²
We've calculated both areas! The square field has an area of 3600 square meters, while the rectangular field has an area of 3200 square meters. Clearly, even though their perimeters were identical, the square field offers a larger total area. This highlights an important concept in geometry!
