Unveiling the Simple Electric Circuit!

How electricity lights up our world.

Hello, young scientists! Today, we're going to explore the exciting world of electricity and learn about a simple electric circuit.
Electric Circuit
A complete path for electricity to flow.
Connects different electrical parts.
Helps devices like bulbs to work.
An electric circuit is like a special path for electricity to travel, making things work, like lighting up a bulb! It needs a continuous path.

Every simple circuit needs a few basic parts: a power source, a device, connecting wires, and a switch. Let's look at each one!

The Flow of Electricity

Step 1
Electricity leaves the battery (+) terminal.
Step 2
Travels through the connecting wire.
Step 3
Passes through the bulb, making it glow.
Step 4
Continues through the other connecting wire.
Step 5
Returns to the battery (-) terminal, completing the circuit.
🔄 Cycle Flow
Imagine electricity as tiny workers moving in a circle! They start from the battery, go through the bulb, and return to the battery, completing their journey.

Open vs. Closed Circuit

Open Circuit
Path is broken or incomplete.
Electricity cannot flow.
Device (e.g., bulb) does NOT work.
Switch is in 'OFF' position.
Closed Circuit
Path is complete and continuous.
Electricity flows freely.
Device (e.g., bulb) WORKS.
Switch is in 'ON' position.
Circuits can be 'closed' or 'open'. When a circuit is closed, the light bulb glows, but when it's open, the bulb is off. Think of a door being open or closed!
