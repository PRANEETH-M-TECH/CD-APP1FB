The Journey to Choose Our Leaders

Understanding Elections in India

Namaste, young citizens! Today, we're going on an exciting journey to understand how we choose our leaders in India through elections.
State Assembly Elections
Purpose: Choose MLAs (Members of Legislative Assembly)
Frequency: Once every five years
Conducted by: Election Commission
Every five years, people in our states get a chance to pick their representatives, called MLAs, for the State Legislative Assembly.

To Contest, a Person MUST:
Be a Citizen of India
Completed 25 years of age
To Contest, a Person MUST NOT:
Hold any 'office of profit' under State/Central Govt.
Lack other qualifications prescribed by law
Not everyone can contest elections. There are important rules about who can stand as a candidate to become an MLA.

Political Party Candidates
Belong to a political party
Represent party's ideology & symbols
Political parties play a major role
Independent Candidates
Do not belong to any political party
Contest on their own (no party affiliation)
Candidates usually belong to political parties, but some individuals choose to contest on their own, known as Independents.
Reaching Voters
Election Manifestos: Descriptions of programmes & promises relevant to local context
Campaigns: Candidates & supporters hold meetings, visit voters door-to-door
Before elections, candidates and parties work hard to tell voters about their plans and promises through manifestos and campaigns.
Voters & Constituencies
Constituency: An area (villages, towns, cities) from which one MLA is elected. Number varies by population (e.g., Hyderabad has 13).
Voters' Role: Get their names registered as voters in their area and vote to elect one member for the Assembly.
For elections, an area is divided into constituencies, and all registered voters in one constituency choose their MLA.

The 5-Year Election Cycle

Step 1
Election Commission calls for elections
Step 2
Candidates (parties & independents) prepare to contest
Step 3
Manifestos released & Campaigns conducted
Step 4
Voters cast their votes in constituencies
Step 5
MLAs are elected for five years
🔄 Cycle Flow
The whole process of elections, from campaigning to voting and choosing MLAs, repeats in a regular cycle every five years.
