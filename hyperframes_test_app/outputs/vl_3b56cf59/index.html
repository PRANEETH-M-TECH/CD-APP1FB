Rani Rudramadevi: The Fearless Warrior Queen

A remarkable ruler of the Kakatiya Dynasty (1262 CE - 1289 CE)

Namaste, young historians! Today, we embark on a journey to meet Rani Rudramadevi, a legendary queen who ruled ancient Telangana. Get ready to discover the story of her bravery and leadership!
Rani Rudramadevi
Rani Rudramadevi belonged to the illustrious Kakatiya family and ruled from their capital, Orugallu, which is modern-day Warangal. She held the throne for a remarkable 27 years!

In our country's history, few women rose to power, and Rani Rudramadevi was one of them! Like Delhi's Raziya Sultana, she faced the challenge of being a woman ruler.
Marco Polo
Even a famous Italian traveler, Marco Polo, visited Rudramadevi's kingdom! He was so impressed that he described her as fearless and strong.
Rani Rudramadevi's Persona
Imagine, she was so impressive that in inscriptions, she was actually known as 'Rudradeva Maharaja', blurring the lines between male and female rulers! She truly broke barriers.

Despite her strength, Rudramadevi constantly faced revolts from chiefs. Tragically, she lost her life fighting one such revolt led by Kayastha Ambadeva in the war at Chandupatla.
