The Process of Elections

How We Choose Our Representatives

Hello Class 8! Today, we're going to explore how we choose our leaders through elections. This is how citizens participate in their government.
First, let's understand who can actually stand for elections. There are specific rules set for individuals who want to become MLAs.

Candidates usually belong to political parties, but some also contest on their own. Let's look at the difference between them.
Before election day, candidates work hard to convince voters. This period is called the election campaign, where they share their plans.
On election day, citizens cast their votes. It's crucial that voting is kept a secret to ensure fairness and prevent pressure.
