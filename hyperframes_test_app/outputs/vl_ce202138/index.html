The President of India: How is S/He Elected?

Understanding the Unique Electoral Process

Hello future citizens! Today, we're going to learn about a very important person in our country: The President of India. Do you know how this powerful position is filled?
President of India
Head of State: The symbolic head of the country.
First Citizen: Represents India on national and international platforms.
Supreme Commander: Leads the Indian Armed Forces.
Appoints Governor: As per your textbook, the President appoints the Governor of a state.
The President is not just a figurehead; they are the Head of State, the first citizen, and the supreme commander of our armed forces! But how do they get chosen?

Who Elects the President?

Unlike general elections where you directly vote for an MLA or MP, the President is elected indirectly by a special body called the Electoral College.
Presidential Election System
Indirect Election: Citizens don't vote directly.
Proportional Representation: Ensures all states have a fair say.
Single Transferable Vote: Voters rank candidates in order of preference.
Secret Ballot: Voting is confidential, just like in general elections you learned about!
The election of the President uses a unique system to ensure fair representation from both states and the central government. It's called Proportional Representation with Single Transferable Vote.

MLA's Vote Value
Depends on the population of their state.
Higher population states have MLAs with higher vote values.
Ensures states are equally represented, not just by number of MLAs.
MP's Vote Value
Calculated based on the total vote value of all MLAs.
All MPs (Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha) have the same vote value.
Ensures balance between state and union representation.
Here's a twist! Not all votes in the Presidential election count equally. The value of an MLA's vote varies from state to state, and an MP's vote has a different value.

Calculating MLA's Vote Value (Simplified)

1
1. Find the total population of the state (from the last census).
2
2. Divide by the total number of elected MLAs in that state.
3
3. Divide the result by 1000 (to make the number manageable).
Let's see a simplified way to understand how an MLA's vote value is calculated. This formula ensures that states with larger populations get more weight.

Calculating MP's Vote Value (Simplified)

1
1. Sum up the vote values of all elected MLAs from all states and UTs.
2
2. Count the total number of elected MPs from both Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.
3
3. Divide the total MLA vote value by the total number of elected MPs.
After calculating the vote values for all MLAs, we can find the vote value for each MP. This makes sure that the total weight of MPs balances the total weight of MLAs.

Why Secret Ballot?
Fairness: Ensures every voter can vote freely without fear.
Prevents Pressure: Protects electors from external influence or intimidation.
Confidentiality: The choice of vote remains private and unknown.
Integrity of Election: A cornerstone of democratic elections, as mentioned in your Social textbook for general voting!
Remember how your textbook mentioned 'secret ballot' for general elections? This principle is equally important in the Presidential election, ensuring fairness and preventing pressure on voters.
