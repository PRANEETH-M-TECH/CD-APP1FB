The Colourful Tests!

Some substances are acids, like vinegar or lemon juice. To find them, we use special things called indicators. Indicators change colour when they meet an acid!

Our Acid Detectives: Litmus & Turmeric

Litmus Paper
Comes in red and blue strips.
Blue litmus turns RED in acid.
Red litmus stays RED in acid.
Turmeric
A yellow powder from your kitchen.
Turmeric paste stays YELLOW in acid.
It turns red in bases (not acids!)
Let's meet two famous indicators: Litmus paper and Turmeric. They both have special colour changes when an acid is nearby!

How To Test For An Acid

Step 1
Take a small amount of the substance.
Step 2
Add a few drops of indicator (e.g., blue litmus solution or turmeric paste).
Step 3
Observe any colour change.
Step 4
If blue litmus turns red, or turmeric stays yellow, it's an acid!
