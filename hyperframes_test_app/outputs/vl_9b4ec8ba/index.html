The Amazing Journey of Paper

From Wood to Wonderful Sheets!

Have you ever wondered how the paper in your books and notebooks is made? Today, we're going on an exciting journey to explore the fascinating process of paper-making!
Paper's Beginning: The Wood
Main Raw Material: Subabul Wood Logs
Arrival at Mill: By Lorries (trucks)
Initial Handling: Lifter Crane to Platform
It all begins with huge logs of wood, often from trees like subabul, arriving at the paper mill. Powerful lifter cranes carefully move these logs, ready for the first stage of transformation!
Stage 1: Chipping
Action: Logs cut into small, even chips
Machine Used: Cutting Machine
Purpose: Prepare wood for chemical treatment
The very first step is called 'Chipping'. Here, the large wood logs are fed into special cutting machines that chop them into small, uniform pieces called wood chips. This makes them easier to process!
Stage 2: Pulping & Whitening
Process: Chips boiled with water & chemicals
Result: Wood transforms into liquid pulp (like cotton fibres)
Refinement: Pulp whitened to creamy-white using chemicals
Next, these wood chips are boiled with water and special chemicals in large tanks. This process breaks down the wood fibres, turning them into a thick, liquid mixture called 'pulp', which is then whitened to a creamy color!
Stage 3: Spreading the Pulp
Action: Liquid pulp spread on thin screens over a cylinder
Key Control: Paper's width, length, and thickness are set here
Transformation: Water drains and evaporates due to heat
The creamy liquid pulp is then spread very thinly and evenly onto special screens that rotate on a cylinder. As the water drains out and evaporates from the heat, the pulp starts to dry and form a delicate, continuous sheet.
Stage 4: Pressing, Drying & Rolling
Smoothing: Drying pulp pressed by heavy rollers
Complete Drying: All water removed to form a firm sheet
Output: Continuous sheet of paper rolled up
The drying pulp sheet is now pressed by large rollers to make it smooth and remove any remaining water. Once completely dry, it becomes a strong, continuous sheet of paper, which is then rolled up into giant paper rolls.
Stage 5: Cutting & Finishing
Action: Paper from rolls cut by machines
Customization: Cut into various sizes (sheets, smaller rolls)
Ready For Use: Packaged and prepared for market
Finally, these huge paper rolls are moved to powerful cutting machines. Here, the paper is cut precisely into the required sizes and shapes, whether it's for notebooks, printing, or other uses, ready to be sent out!
