--- Generated Answer ---
Hi everyone! Acids are special substances that usually taste very sour, just like lemon juice, curd, or vinegar. If you test an acid using a special indicator, like a blue litmus paper strip, the acid will make that blue paper change color to red. We find some acids naturally in food, like the acid in an ant sting, but other acids, like Hydrochloric acid, are very strong chemicals made in labs and must never be touched or tasted.
