### Usage Patterns

While we can use all of these parts, the textbook notes that some are more widely used than others:

*   **Widely Used Parts:** Leaves, roots, seeds, and fruits of plants are commonly and widely consumed in our food.
*   **Less Widely Used Parts:** Stems and flowers are also used, but not as widely as the other four categories. For example, some plants, like sugarcane or ginger, have modified stems that we eat, and we also consume certain flowers.

*   **Roots:** We eat the roots of plants like Carrot, Radish, and Beetroot.
*   **Stems:** We consume modified stems like Potato, Ginger, and Sugarcane.
*   **Leaves and Seeds:** For a plant like Fenugreek (Menthulu), we eat both its leaves and its seeds.
